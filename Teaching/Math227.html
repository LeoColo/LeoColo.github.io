Professor: Leonardo Colò
Program: Honours Physics
Course code: MATH 227
Section: 001
Workload: Sept-Dec 2024 (12 weeks)
Class Schedule: MWF 11h30 - 12h30 in MC1085

SYLLABUS

  1. Multivariable Vector Functions and Line Integrals (3 weeks):
      - Introduction to Multivariable Vector Calculus.
      - Linear Objects in \(\mathbb{R}^3\).
      - Regions in \(\mathbb{R}^n\).
      - Curves and particle motion.
      - Arc Length.
      - Line Integrals.
  2. Multiple Integrals (2 semaine):
    • - Cylinders and Quadric Surfaces.
      - Coordinate Systems.
      - Double integrals.
      - Triple integrals.
  3. Gradients and Directional Derivatives (2 weeks):
    • - Partial Derivatives.
      - Linearization.
      - Chain Rule.
      - Directional Derivative and Gradient.
  4. Vector Fields (4 weeks):
    • - Introduction to vector fields.
      - Divergence and Curl.
      - Path Independence of Line Integrals.
      - Green's Theorem.
      - Surface integrals.
      - Stokes' Theorem.
      - Divergence Theorem.
  5. Optimization (1 week):
    • - Extreme Values.
      - Lagrange Multipliers
