In my research I investigate questions related to supersingular elliptic curves, their endomorphism ring and associated isogeny graphs. I am particularly interested in: cryptography, elliptic curves, abelian varieties, algorithms for number theory, canonical lifts of elliptic curves, class field theory and modular curves.
